"FACTS ABOUT DOGS"

Dogs are descendants of wolves and were the first species domesticated by humans over 20,000 years ago, evolving into diverse breeds with highly sensitive senses. They possess a sense of smell up to 100,000 times better than humans, unique nose prints, and are roughly as intelligent as a two-year-old child.

Scientific studies (cynology) reveal that dogs possess remarkable social intelligence, capable of understanding human nonverbal cues, emotions, and even thoughts, often acting as partners rather than just pets. Research shows they form deep, supportive bonds similar to parent-child relationships, with mutual oxytocin increases during bonding. Their evolution, driven by cooperation with humans, has made them highly attuned to, and dependent on, human communication.
